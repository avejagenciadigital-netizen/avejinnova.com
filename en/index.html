Frequently asked questions

Before you start

How are the proposals made?

We start from your photo and measurements. The visual proposals are generated with AI, and our team prepares the plan, materials list and budget for the option you choose.

How soon will I get them?

Idea Express in 48 hours, Kit Local Pro in 5 business days and Premium in 4 business days, counted from when we receive your photo, measurements and payment. For large projects the timeline depends on size and we confirm it in the quote.

Why does the price depend on my country?

We adjust prices to each region's market: Latin America, Europe, and the US and Canada. The service is exactly the same. Choose your region above the plans.

Do you work in my city?

Proposals and plans are delivered online to any country. Build-out depends on whether we have professionals in your city; message us and we'll confirm.

How do I pay?

With each plan's button: you pay on Hotmart by card or local methods, then send us your photos and details. If you prefer PayPal or Binance, message us on WhatsApp and we'll send the details.

My shop is tiny. Does this still work?

That's where it shows most. In a 10 × 13 ft shop, moving the counter or swapping chairs for a bench can add several seats or put your products in view from the street.

I don't have much budget to renovate.

That's why every proposal includes an estimated budget and materials list. Start with the change that sells most (usually the display or the layout) and do the rest in stages.

Do I have to close my business?

Not to get the proposals: we only need a photo and measurements. For the build-out, the plan lets you schedule the work over a few days or in parts, without closing.

Can my own contractor do the work?

Yes. The plan and materials list are designed so any contractor can follow them. If you don't have one, we connect you with the AVEJ network in your city.

Does it work for my type of business?

We work with barbershops, salons, bakeries, coffee shops, restaurants, stores, hardware stores and clothing boutiques. If yours is different, message us and we'll tell you before you pay.

What is the virtual tour?

A scene-by-scene walkthrough of your renovated space using the proposal you picked. You open it from a link on your phone and can compare each view with the real photo. It's an add-on to your Kit and is included in Premium.

What if I don't like any proposal?

Message us on WhatsApp and we'll adjust the closest one at no cost. Kit Local Pro includes 1 revision and Premium includes 2.

Do you take on large projects?

Yes: large homes, multi-area businesses, offices and residential developments. Fill in the large projects form and we'll send you a custom quote on WhatsApp.

Do you sell plans for the pieces in your reels?

Yes. If you saw a piece in our reels, ask for that plan on WhatsApp.

Do you do the build-out?

We connect your project with the AVEJ professional network in your city, using the plan and budget you already have.

Do you work in construction or carpentry?

Join the AVEJ professional network. We coordinate projects based on demand in your city and pay you for your work.
